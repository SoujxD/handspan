════ Why some detectors stay UNVERIFIED, honestly

13 of 19 declared detectors fire against the live application. The six that do
not fall into two groups, and neither is evidence that the detector is wrong —
only that this pass could not reach the state.

  session_timeout / session_timed_out / system_error / settings_area_off_limits
      These need a fault injected at a step the flow reaches by CLICKING, not by
      navigating. `?inject=` is a URL parameter, so it can only be attached to a
      navigation — and the only navigation in these capabilities is the entry
      URL, before the state matters. The alternative is the System Settings
      screen, which sets injection GLOBALLY on a host shared with other
      candidates. Breaking someone else's demo to tick a box here is not a
      trade worth making.

  invalid_initial_deposit
      Verified as shadowed rather than broken. The application's generic
      "could not be validated" banner matches request_not_validated first, and
      outcomes match in declaration order. Both are now classified `business`,
      so the shadowing is harmless: a rejected deposit is reported as an answer
      either way. Reordering would need a re-recording; reclassifying was the
      cheaper correct fix.

  validation_errors
      Same shadowing shape as above, behind invalid_email_format.

What would close the first group: a per-step fault hook in the replay engine
that can attach `?inject=` to the request a click is about to make. That is a
core change for the benefit of a test, so it is on the next-steps list rather
than in this build.
